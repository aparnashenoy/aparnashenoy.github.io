Container stacking optimisation

Mixed-integer programme (Gurobi) that assigns 100–200 containers to yard slots, minimising expected crane rehandles under uncertain retrieval order. Group-aggregated formulation keeps model size independent of container count; a Monte Carlo simulator with three retrieval policies checks the blocking-probability proxy against actual moves. 67% fewer expected rehandles than a tuned greedy baseline, solved to proven optimality in 1.3s.
